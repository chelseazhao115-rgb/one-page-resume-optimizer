# Final Resume Check

## Pagination

- [ ] The final rendered resume is exactly one page.
- [ ] There is no clipping, overlap, or hidden overflow.

## Content-bearing item tail lines

- [ ] Multi-line product/project items have final-line utilization >=60%.
- [ ] Multi-line work/internship/relevant-experience items have final-line utilization >=60%.
- [ ] Multi-line research items have final-line utilization >=60%.
- [ ] The preferred 65%–85% range is used where it improves balance.
- [ ] No filler was added merely to stretch a line.

The minimum does not apply to skills/tool lists, languages, honors, contact information, course lists, titles, dates, or similar metadata.

## Visual preservation and readability

- [ ] Colors, bold, italics, bullets, icons, hyperlinks, alignment, photos, and custom macros/styles are preserved.
- [ ] Native XeLaTeX/Overleaf or Word structure is preserved.
- [ ] Body text is not unreasonably small.
- [ ] Line spacing and section spacing are not visibly crushed.
- [ ] Margins remain professional and readable.

## Content integrity and JD relevance

- [ ] Core experiences and hard evidence are retained.
- [ ] Any removed or materially deprioritized core content is disclosed.
- [ ] No metrics, skills, responsibilities, or outcomes are invented.
- [ ] When a JD is provided, high-relevance evidence is retained and prioritized.
- [ ] Low-relevance content is compressed or removed before high-relevance content.
